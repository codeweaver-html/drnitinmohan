Why Dr. Nitin Mohan

Why Dr. Nitin Mohan

1. Specialist expertise in paediatric digestive and liver disorders

Children have unique medical and nutritional needs. Dr. Nitin Mohan focuses on evaluating digestive, liver and nutritional conditions in the context of a child’s age, growth, development and overall health.

2. A systematic approach to diagnosis

Recurrent abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, constipation, diarrhoea, jaundice and poor weight gain can have several underlying causes. Dr. Nitin Mohan aims to identify the likely cause through careful clinical assessment and appropriate investigations.

3. A focus on growth and nutrition

Digestive health is closely linked to a child’s growth and development. Assessment may include dietary habits, growth patterns, feeding difficulties and nutritional requirements to help develop an appropriate treatment plan.

4. Evidence-based, individualised treatment

Every child is different. Dr. Nitin Mohan aims to provide treatment guided by clinical findings, relevant investigations and established medical recommendations, with follow-up tailored to each child’s needs.

5. Clear communication with parents

Understanding a child’s illness is an important part of treatment. Dr. Nitin Mohan emphasises explaining possible causes, recommended tests, treatment options and follow-up requirements in language parents can understand.

6. Specialist investigations when indicated

Selected digestive disorders may require investigations such as upper gastrointestinal endoscopy, colonoscopy, flexible sigmoidoscopy or gastrointestinal manometry, depending on the child’s symptoms and clinical findings. These are recommended when medically appropriate and subject to availability.

7. Commitment to academic and clinical development

Dr. Nitin Mohan’s professional background includes scientific publications, clinical audit and academic presentations, reflecting a commitment to continued learning and evidence-based clinical practice.

A Commitment to Your Child’s Wellbeing

Dr. Nitin Mohan aims to help families understand their child’s condition, receive an appropriate treatment plan and make informed decisions about their care, with attention to safety, comfort, nutrition and long-term wellbeing.

At Churamani Multispeciality Hospital, Hisar, families can seek specialist consultation for children with digestive, liver, pancreatic and nutritional concerns.
