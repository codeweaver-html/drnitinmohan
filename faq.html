Frequently asked questions

How can I book an appointment?

Select Book an appointment and choose Book via WhatsApp or Book via RezeptRx. For WhatsApp booking, enter your child’s name and age, select a preferred date and an available 30-minute time slot, then choose Continue to WhatsApp. The floating WhatsApp button opens the same booking form.

What are the clinic hours?

The booking form displays available 30-minute appointment slots after you select a date. Select a date to see the available times, or choose Contact Us in the main navigation to jump to the homepage footer and call the clinic.

Where is the clinic?

Churamani Multispeciality Hospital, Delhi Road, Camp Chowk, Krishna Nagar Colony, Hisar, Haryana 125001. Use View location in the homepage footer to open Google Maps for directions.

How can I contact the clinic?

Select Contact Us in the main navigation to jump directly to the homepage footer, where the clinic phone number and Google Maps link are available. You can also use the footer or the floating WhatsApp button to start an appointment request.

13. What should I bring to the consultation?

Bring previous prescriptions, test reports, imaging reports, discharge summaries, current medication details and your child’s growth records, if available. A brief record of symptoms, diet and bowel habits may also help.

14. Are all investigations and procedures available at the hospital?

Availability depends on the investigation, procedure, the child’s age and clinical requirements. Contact the hospital to ask about the specific service you need.

15. How can I book an appointment?

Use the Book an appointment button or the floating WhatsApp button on this website to send an appointment request.
