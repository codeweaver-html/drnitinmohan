Digestive system

Paediatric Gastroenterology

Conditions affecting the oesophagus, stomach and intestines.

Digestive symptoms and common conditions

  • Recurrent abdominal pain
  • Chronic constipation and faecal soiling
  • Acute and chronic diarrhoea
  • Recurrent vomiting and nausea
  • Gastro-oesophageal reflux disease (GERD/GORD)
  • Abdominal bloating and excessive gas
  • Poor appetite and feeding difficulties
  • Gastrointestinal infections and worm infestations

Specific gastrointestinal disorders

  • Coeliac disease
  • Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS)
  • Cow’s milk protein allergy and other food allergies
  • Gastritis and peptic ulcer disease
  • Crohn’s disease and ulcerative colitis (IBD)
  • Very-early-onset IBD (VEO-IBD)
  • Eosinophilic oesophagitis and other eosinophilic gastrointestinal disorders
  • Gastrointestinal bleeding
  • Malabsorption and protein-losing enteropathy
  • Gastrointestinal motility disorders
  • Congenital gastrointestinal abnormalities

Liver and bile processing

Paediatric Hepatology — Liver Diseases

Conditions affecting liver function, liver tissue and the body’s ability to process nutrients and waste products.

Jaundice, hepatitis and liver function

  • Jaundice and abnormal liver function tests
  • Hepatitis A, B, C, D and E
  • Drug-induced liver injury
  • Autoimmune hepatitis
  • Chronic hepatitis and chronic liver disease
  • Fatty liver disease (MASLD)
  • Neonatal hepatitis and neonatal cholestasis

Other liver conditions

  • Wilson disease
  • Portal hypertension and oesophageal or gastric varices
  • Liver fibrosis and cirrhosis
  • Acute liver failure
  • Enlarged liver or spleen associated with liver disease
  • Glycogen storage diseases
  • Alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency
  • Progressive familial intrahepatic cholestasis (PFIC)
  • Alagille syndrome
  • Autoimmune sclerosing cholangitis
  • Other genetic, metabolic and inherited liver diseases
  • Unexplained liver inflammation
  • Assessment and referral for liver transplantation when indicated

Conditions affecting the gallbladder, bile ducts and the flow of bile from the liver to the intestine.

  • Cholangitis and biliary infections

Healthy development

Paediatric Nutrition and Growth

Conditions affecting a child’s growth, feeding and nutritional health.

  • Poor weight gain and growth faltering
  • Undernutrition and malnutrition
  • Iron, vitamin and other nutritional deficiencies
  • Feeding difficulties and restricted food intake
  • Nutritional problems associated with chronic diarrhoea or malabsorption
  • Dietary management of coeliac disease and selected food allergies
  • Nutritional support in chronic liver and pancreatic diseases
  • Growth monitoring and individualised nutrition planning

Guidance for parents

When should parents seek specialist consultation?

Consider a specialist consultation for persistent abdominal pain, recurrent vomiting, chronic diarrhoea, blood in stools, poor weight gain, prolonged jaundice, pale stools or unexplained abnormal liver function tests.

Seek urgent medical assessment

Urgent assessment is important for green vomiting, significant gastrointestinal bleeding, severe abdominal pain, marked dehydration, unusual drowsiness or jaundice accompanied by concerning symptoms. Jaundice with pale stools or dark urine in a young infant requires prompt evaluation.

The conditions listed are areas for clinical assessment. The need for consultation, investigations or treatment depends on each child’s symptoms and clinical findings.
