Services

Paediatric investigations and services

Dr. Nitin Mohan provides specialist paediatric gastroenterology, hepatology, pancreatic and biliary consultations at Churamani Multispeciality Hospital, Hisar. Relevant diagnostic investigations and procedures help evaluate digestive disorders, liver diseases, pancreatic conditions and biliary tract abnormalities in children.

1. Paediatric Gastrointestinal Procedures

  • Upper Gastrointestinal Endoscopy (UGIE)
  • Colonoscopy
  • Proctosigmoidoscopy
  • Endoscopic Variceal Ligation (EVL)
  • Endoscopic Dilatation
  • Double-Balloon Enteroscopy
  • Gastrointestinal Manometry
  • Oesophageal pH Monitoring (pH Metry)
  • Biofeedback Therapy

2. Paediatric Hepatology Procedures and Investigations

  • FibroScan (Transient Elastography)
  • Liver Function Tests (LFT)
  • Total and Direct Bilirubin
  • AST (SGOT), ALT (SGPT), ALP and GGT
  • Serum Albumin and Total Protein
  • Serum Bile Acids
  • Hepatitis A and E IgM
  • Hepatitis B Surface Antigen and HBV DNA Quantification
  • Hepatitis C Antibody and HCV RNA Quantification
  • Liver Biopsy and Histopathological Examination
  • Autoimmune Liver Disease Investigations

3. Pancreatic Investigations

  • Serum Amylase
  • Serum Lipase
  • CT Pancreatic Protocol, when clinically indicated

4. Biliary Tract Investigations

  • Ultrasound Abdomen for hepatobiliary assessment
  • Abdominal Ultrasound with Doppler, when indicated
  • Liver Function and Bilirubin Assessment
  • Serum Bile Acids
  • Selected Contrast Gastrointestinal Studies, when indicated

5. Gastrointestinal Diagnostic Investigations

  • Stool Routine Examination
  • Stool Occult Blood Test
  • Stool Culture and Sensitivity
  • Tissue Transglutaminase IgA (tTG-IgA) for Coeliac Disease
  • Total IgA
  • Histopathological Examination of Gastrointestinal Biopsy Specimens
  • Complete Blood Count (CBC), CRP and ESR when indicated
  • Vitamin B12, Vitamin D and Ferritin for selected nutritional assessments

6. Paediatric Nutrition and Growth Assessment

  • Weight, Height and Growth-Chart Assessment
  • Evaluation of Growth Faltering and Poor Weight Gain
  • Nutritional Assessment in Chronic Gastrointestinal, Liver and Pancreatic Diseases
  • Investigation of Suspected Nutritional Deficiencies
  • Individualised Nutrition Planning and Follow-up

Important Information

Investigations and procedures are selected according to the child’s symptoms, clinical assessment and suspected diagnosis. Availability may vary by procedure and the child’s age and clinical requirements. Please contact Churamani Multispeciality Hospital to confirm current availability.
